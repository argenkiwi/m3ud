Ambler node IDs have fixed edges, so a step that the spec says continues to different places (Save M3U: to PROMPT_DOWNLOAD after resolve, terminate after download) is wired twice from the same factory (SAVE_M3U_FILE and SAVE_LOCAL_M3U_FILE). khinsider track pages are resolved by taking the first absolute .mp3 href that is not on downloads.khinsider.com, because the canonical <link> also ends in .mp3 and would otherwise match first. khinsider sits behind Cloudflare and returned a 403 challenge page to automated fetches (even with a browser UA) from the dev sandbox, so live resolution was unverified at build time.
